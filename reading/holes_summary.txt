HOLES — CHAPTERS 1–20 SUMMARY

Stanley Yelnats is wrongly convicted of stealing sneakers belonging to the famous baseball player Clyde “Sweet Feet” Livingston. The shoes had fallen from an overpass and struck Stanley, but nobody believes his explanation. Stanley blames the bad luck that seems to follow his family and chooses to attend Camp Green Lake instead of going to jail.

Camp Green Lake is actually a dry, barren wasteland. Stanley is assigned to Tent D, where he meets boys known as X-Ray, Armpit, Squid, Zigzag, Magnet, and Zero. The camp is controlled by the Warden, Mr. Sir, and the counselor Mr. Pendanski.

Every day, each boy must dig a hole five feet deep and five feet wide. The adults claim that digging builds character, but Stanley gradually suspects that they are searching for something. A boy who discovers an interesting object may earn the rest of the day off.

Stanley initially struggles with the heat, thirst, blisters, and physical labor, but digging gradually makes him stronger. He also learns the camp’s social rules, particularly that X-Ray is the group’s leader.

A flashback explains the supposed Yelnats family curse. Stanley’s ancestor Elya Yelnats promised Madame Zeroni that he would carry her up a mountain after she helped him. He forgot to fulfill the promise, supposedly bringing misfortune upon his descendants.

Stanley first discovers a fish fossil, but Mr. Pendanski says it is not valuable enough to earn a reward. He later finds a small gold-colored tube engraved with the initials “K.B.” Stanley gives it to X-Ray because X-Ray has been at camp longer and wants a day off. X-Ray waits until the next morning and pretends to discover it in a different location.

The Warden becomes intensely interested in the tube and orders the boys to expand their search around X-Ray’s hole. Her reaction confirms Stanley’s suspicion that the digging is really a search for something she wants.

Zero reveals that he cannot read or write and asks Stanley to teach him. Stanley initially refuses because he is exhausted after digging all day.

Later, Magnet steals Mr. Sir’s sunflower seeds and passes the bag among the boys. The seeds spill into Stanley’s hole, and Stanley accepts the blame to protect the others. Mr. Sir takes him to the Warden. Instead of punishing Stanley, the Warden—wearing nail polish containing rattlesnake venom—strikes Mr. Sir across the face and says she does not care about his sunflower seeds.

By Chapter 20, the central mystery is clear: the Warden is using the boys to search beneath the dried lake, and the object marked “K.B.” may be an important clue.
